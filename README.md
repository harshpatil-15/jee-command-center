# JEE Command Center 

A personal, modern dashboard for preparing for the **JEE (Joint Entrance Examination)**.

JEE Command Center is designed to turn JEE preparation into a measurable system — combining syllabus tracking, spaced repetition, study analytics, focus sessions, and progress tracking in one place.

> Built as a personal project to make my own JEE preparation more organized, measurable, and consistent.

---

##  Features

###  Dashboard
- Overall syllabus completion
- Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics progress
- Visual completion chart
- Weekly study activity
- Study streak
- Revision overview
- Recently completed chapters
- Weak and unfinished chapters
- Personal JEE Readiness metric

###  Syllabus Tracker
- Complete JEE syllabus organized by subject and section
- Chapter-level progress tracking
- Chapter status:
  - Not Started
  - Learning
  - Completed
  - Needs Revision
  - Mastered
- Confidence tracking
- Difficulty tracking
- Notes
- Search, filtering, and sorting

###  Spaced Repetition
An Anki-inspired revision system designed specifically for JEE preparation.

Revision intervals include:
- 1 day
- 3 days
- 7 days
- 14 days
- 30 days
- 60 days
- 90 days
- Custom dates

Revision feedback:
- Again
- Hard
- Good
- Easy

Revision history is tracked so progress can be reviewed over time.

### 📈 Analytics
Track preparation over time with:
- Overall progress
- Subject comparisons
- Section performance
- Completion velocity
- Study activity
- Revision activity
- Weak and strong areas
- Estimated syllabus completion

###  Focus Timer
Built-in study timer with customizable sessions.

Default Pomodoro sessions:
- 25 min focus
- 5 min break
- 15 min long break

Study sessions can be associated with chapters and contribute to analytics.
